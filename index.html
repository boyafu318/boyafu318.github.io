When does an “aha”
reflect understanding?

My ongoing honors thesis examines whether self-reported ‘aha’ moments during STEM lectures correspond to accurate understanding and improved learning performance.

Institution
University of Chicago
Supervisor
Monica Rosenberg
Focus
Insight, metacognition, learning

The question

A compelling feeling.
A testable distinction.

Do self-reported insights during STEM lectures correspond to accurate conceptual understanding and better performance on questions about those concepts?

The project considers prior knowledge when examining the relationship between reported insight and learning.

Research approach

  1. Identify an insightExamine moments when learners report an “aha” during a lecture.
  2. Examine the explanationEvaluate what the learner says they understood.
  3. Connect it to assessmentRelate the explanation to responses about the same concept.
Work in progress. This page describes the research question and approach. Findings and shareable research materials will be added as the project develops.

I’m Boya, a Cognitive Science undergraduate at the University of Chicago.

My current research, supervised by Monica Rosenberg, examines the relationship between the experience of insight and evidence of learning. I hope to connect this work with the design and evaluation of human–AI interactions.

I’m interested in a future where AI supports curiosity, independent judgment, and the ability to recognize what we do not yet understand.
